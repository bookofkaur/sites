Real Essays, Annotated

By Darrian Belcher2 min readApplications · Essays

Advice about what makes a great essay only gets you so far — at some point you need to see one. These are real essays I wrote for my own applications, the ones that helped bring in 25 acceptances, 10+ full rides, and $1M+ in scholarships. Nothing here is a template to copy. It's a look at the actual choices — the hook, the specific details, the pivot from struggle to purpose — broken down so you can make those same choices in your own story.

Each one shows the prompt, the essay exactly as I submitted it, and inline notes on craft, voice, and structure — plus a "what made this work" checklist at the end you can hold your own draft up against.

  • The McDonald's Essay — Short Personal Statement

    The essay that opens with "Apron? Check. Work hat? Check." — turning a house fire and a part-time job into a story about what actually matters.

  • The STEM Mentorship Essay — Community Impact

    Why representation in tech matters to me, and the concrete plan I built to do something about it.

  • The "Why This School" Essay — Carnegie Mellon

    A short, specific supplemental essay connecting a self-taught coding habit to one school's actual program.

💡 Read these after you've tried Step 3 of How to Find Your Essay Topic. Examples work best once you already have a rough story of your own to compare them against — otherwise it's too easy to just borrow mine.
