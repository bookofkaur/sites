Before you trust any date here

Colleges set their own deadlines and occasionally change them. These are the fixed, program-wide dates for the 2026-27 cycle — always confirm the exact deadline on each college's own admissions page, especially the time of day. When a date shows "time varies by college," we count down to the earliest moment it could close so you're never caught short.
