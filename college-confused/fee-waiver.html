Can You Apply for Free?

College application fees run $50–90 each. Apply to ten schools and that's $500–900 — money a lot of families simply don't have. Most students who qualify to skip those fees never find out. Takes about a minute.

    What a fee waiver actually gets you

    • Every Common App college

      One waiver covers all the colleges you apply to through Common App. There's no cap on how many.

    • Nobody can tell

      Colleges don't see your waiver when reading your application. It has no effect on your chances of getting in.

    • Tests too, separately

      SAT and ACT have their own fee waivers, and an SAT/ACT waiver by itself qualifies you for the Common App one.

    • Not on Common App?

      Ask about the NACAC waiver form, or apply directly — plenty of colleges charge no application fee at all.

    Where this comes from

    The nine criteria above are the official eligibility indicators on the Common App fee waiver form, reproduced in plain English. You self-report that you meet one, then your counselor or another school official signs off.
