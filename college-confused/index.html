    Why this site is different

    We don't want your data

    Most "free" college sites make their money by selling your information to colleges and advertisers, or by running the same test you're studying for. We don't do any of that — there is nothing to sign up for, because there is nothing we want from you.

    No accounts

    Nothing to sign up for. No email, no password, no profile.

    No trackers or ads

    No analytics, no advertising pixels, nobody following you around the web afterward.

    Your work stays yours

    Essays and checklists are saved in your own browser. Your essay only leaves your device when you press the AI feedback button.

    No one pays us to say this

    No college sponsors us and no scholarship company pays for placement. Cost numbers come straight from public federal data.
