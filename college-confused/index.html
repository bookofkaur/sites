Built by someone who's been there

I'm Darrian Belcher. Growing up, my house burned down, my parents split, and I worked weekends at McDonald's to help my family stay afloat. I taught myself to code on a school-issued Chromebook because it was the only computer I had. My older sister had already been through the process and helped me every step of the way — from the Common App to essays to FAFSA. Not every student has that person. This site is my attempt to be that person for the ones who don't.
