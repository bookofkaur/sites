Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

The short version

College Confused is a static content site — guides and articles, nothing more. There are no accounts, no forms to fill out, no analytics trackers, and no ads. It's a free nonprofit resource, and we don't collect anything from you to run it.

What we don't have

There's no login, no signup, no data entry of any kind on this site. We don't operate a server that stores information about who visits or what they read. If you've used College Confused before and remember an in-browser toolkit that saved your timeline, scholarships, or essay drafts to your device — that tool has retired. Its guidance now lives here as plain guides, and nothing about your college process is stored anywhere by us.

Children's privacy

College Confused is designed for students 13 and up. Because we collect nothing from anyone, there is no personal information from any user — of any age — on our servers.
